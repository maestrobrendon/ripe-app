Basket homepage hero illustration
=================================

Generated 2026-10-10 for the homepage hero (src/app/home-menu/parts/hero.tsx).

Tool:   Playground AI (playgroundai.com), via its MCP connector, free plan
Model:  GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst ("gpt-image-2.5-sunburst")
Output: 1536 x 1024 PNG (the model's maximum; 2560 x 1440 was requested)

Chosen concept: 1, "Shawarma table" (concepts/c1-shawarma.webp)
Other concepts, same tool and model: concepts/c2-market.webp, concepts/c3-smoothie.webp
Concept 4 ("Cooking for four") was not generated: Playground's free credits ran
out, Comfy Cloud needs a paid subscription for partner models, and the Recraft
and Higgsfield connectors could not generate on this account.

Prompt (concept 1)
------------------
Flat vector editorial food illustration, top-down tabletop view, bold simple
shapes, each object one base colour with one darker shadow tone and a hard
offset drop shadow, small confetti flecks of herbs and seeds, crisp edges, no
gradients, no outlines, no text, no letters, no logos. Objects crop off the
canvas edges; the centre of the canvas is left completely empty. Wide 16:9
hero banner. Flat pale sky-blue field (#dceeff); hard drop shadows in a deeper
dusty blue (#a9c8e6). Restrained palette: carbon black, white, mint green
#55db9c, ember orange #fb4903, sunburst yellow #ffd731, lavender #e9ccff. Top
right corner, cropped by the edges: a chicken shawarma bowl on a patterned
cloth. Bottom left corner, cropped: a large plate of green mango salad. Bottom
right corner: a dark-skinned hand reaching in from the edge holding a lime
wedge. Small bowls of yoghurt sauce and chilli; scattered lime slices and
parsley leaves near the edges. The middle 45% of the width and 70% of the
height is completely empty flat blue. Mature, sophisticated magazine
illustration, not childish, no faces on food.

Post-processing (local, no further AI)
--------------------------------------
- layers/*.webp: the generated image cut into 11 transparent layers with a
  colour key on the flat field (#d1eafd, measured from the art). Edge pixels
  are un-premultiplied against the field so they sit cleanly on it; each dish
  keeps its own drop shadow. layers.json lists every layer's box in the
  1536 x 1024 source.
- The background field is not an image: it is the CSS colour #d1eafd on the
  hero section (--hm-field in src/app/home-menu/home-menu.css).
- In the live hero the yoghurt bowl is tucked further into its corner and the
  chilli bowl moves from top centre to the bottom right, so both clear the
  headline and the nav. hero.tsx holds the live positions.
- hero-desktop.webp (1920 x 1080, 16:9) and hero-mobile.webp (1080 x 1920,
  9:16) are stills flattened from the layers in that live arrangement, for
  social cards, decks and other places without the animated hero.
